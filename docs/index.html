About Us

ILISY Golf and Country Club is a premier private golf destination offering world-class facilities, meticulously maintained greens, and an atmosphere of refined leisure. Founded with a passion for the sport, we welcome members who share our commitment to excellence.

Our championship-grade course spans lush, manicured fairways designed to challenge and inspire golfers of every skill level. Beyond the greens, members enjoy access to a full-service clubhouse, spa and wellness facilities, fine dining, and a vibrant social calendar — all within a secure, private environment that puts comfort and exclusivity first.
